Weekly Instructor Report — Metric Definitions

Every metric in your weekly email, in plain words — what it means and what it counts.

Scorecard

The overall teaching score, out of 77 (shown as a %).

MetricDefinitionConsiderations
Teaching Score / 35 How well lectures were taught — combines QA rating, teaching quality, quiz scores and student feedback. Lecture sessions in the week. Blank if any part has no data.
Tutorial Score / 35 How well practice sessions went — combines feedback, benchmark sessions and assessment clearance. Practice sessions in the week. Blank if any part has no data.
Preparedness Score / 7 How ready the class was — the pre-class readiness signal. Based on pre-class setup and checklist.
Overall Score / 77 Teaching + Tutorial + Preparedness, shown as a percentage. Needs all three areas to have data. Culture is planned but not live, so the total is 77 today.
Performance Band The overall % placed into a simple band (see below). "No score" means one area had no data that week — not a bad score.

Deviations (the 12 flags)

A name appears only if it crossed the line — shown as Name - value.

MetricDefinitionConsiderations
Company Mic Usage Below 90%
< 90%		 How often the company mic was used across sessions. Lecture & Practice sessions that have already ended.
QA Report Open Rate Below 80%
< 80%		 How many of the instructor's QA reports were opened. Lecture & Practice sessions that have a QA report generated.
Average QA Score Below 6.5
< 6.5 / 10		 The instructor's average class-quality score. Lecture & Practice sessions.
Pre-Lecture Readiness Below 70%
< 70%		 Average completion of the pre-class checklist. Lecture (8 items) & Practice (7 items).
Session Completion Rate Below 90%
< 90%		 How many scheduled sessions were marked completed. Lecture, Practice & Exam sessions.
Portal Recording Adoption Below 60%
< 60%		 How many sessions were recorded through the TeachOS portal. Lecture & Practice sessions that have already started.
Session Not Started Within 10 Minutes
> 0 sessions		 Sessions that started more than 10 min after the scheduled time. All session types with a recording start time.
Instructor Late in More Than 3 Sessions
> 3 sessions		 Sessions that started more than 15 min late. Lecture & Practice sessions.
Instructor No-Show
> 0 sessions		 Sessions never started — no recording, no evaluation, still pending. Lecture, Practice & Exam sessions.
Completed Session With No QA Report
> 0 sessions		 Completed sessions with no QA report attached. Completed Lecture, Practice & Exam sessions.
Session Not Marked Completed
> 0 sessions		 Sessions past end time still pending, started, or not marked. Lecture, Practice & Exam sessions.
Zero Attendance Marked
> 0 sessions		 Sessions where no student was marked present. Scheduled session slots with student attendance data.
For percentage flags the value is a rate (Name - 72%); for the rest it's a count (Name - 3 sessions). Names are listed worst-first. No names under a flag means nobody breached it.
